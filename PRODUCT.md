## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring companies evaluating Alessandra Mineo's UX/UI design and front-end experience.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents selected design and front-end work, expertise, tools, and contact information so hiring companies can assess fit and get in touch.

## Positioning

The existing homepage describes more than 20 years of experience turning complex requirements into clear, usable digital experiences, combining UX thinking, visual craft, and front-end knowledge.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is an existing Hugo web portfolio.
- Preserve the current homepage content and factual claims in concept explorations; do not invent credentials, outcomes, clients, or project details.
- Keep concept work separate from the production homepage unless the user approves a direction for implementation.

## Brand Commitments

- The portfolio is named cherry.web.
- Keep existing factual copy and contact details unchanged in redesign concepts.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing homepage and portfolio source: `index.html`, `assets/js/work-list.js`, and the project pages under `projects/`.
- Project thumbnails and other portfolio imagery under `assets/images/portfolio/`.

## Product Principles

- Make relevant work and capabilities easy for hiring companies to evaluate.
- Ground project descriptions and experience claims in the existing portfolio.
- Make it straightforward to contact Alessandra.
