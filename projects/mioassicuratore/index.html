Overview

Mio Assicuratore simplifies insurance management for both clients and agents - covering policy management, onboarding, and customer interactions across an agent intranet and a client-facing web app.

01 - Project Vision & Strategy

The platform serves two distinct users - agents managing clients and policies, and customers purchasing and monitoring coverage - requiring separate but visually consistent experiences for each.

02 - UX Research & Concept Development

Collaborated with the development team to define user flows and wireframes for both products - focusing on simplifying complex insurance information and reducing friction across onboarding, policy management, and purchases.

03 - Design Process & Prototyping

Built high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes validated with internal stakeholders, delivering:

  • New logo and mascot design
  • Responsive web app for policy management and purchases
  • Consistent visual identity across intranet, client platform, and marketing campaigns

04 - My Role

Led design across both products, including:

  • Logo and mascot design
  • Agent intranet panel design
  • Onboarding flows in collaboration with the Angular development team
  • High-fidelity mockups, prototypes, and client web app UI
