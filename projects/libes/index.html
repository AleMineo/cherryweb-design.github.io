UX/UI design - HTML/SCSS - Logo designLibes - Social Network Platform

Libes is a platform born from the idea of sharing geographic and cultural knowledge as an alternative to traditional social networks. Its goal was to engage travelers and explorers, offering insights into both iconic destinations and hidden gems, powered by a responsible and active community.

01 - Product Vision & Strategy

Libes is a travel social platform built around user-generated content and community discovery - enabling ambassadors to create POI pages, explore destinations on an interactive global map, and share authentic travel insights.

02 - UX Research & Concept Development

Wireframed user flows for content creation, map interaction, and social engagement - focusing on what motivates travelers to share, and how to balance open contribution with quality control.

03 - Design Process & Prototyping

Built high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes for core platform features, including personal spaces where users could:

  • Upload and geolocate photos on a global map
  • Create travel blogs, itineraries, and recommendations
  • Engage with the community while maintaining a curated personal collection

04 - Monetization & Future Features

Designed around a monetization strategy integrating travel services (Booking.com, Skyscanner) and commercial pages for agencies, accommodations, and cruise operators - building a sustainable ecosystem without compromising the discovery experience.

05 - My Role

Led UX/UI design end-to-end, including:

  • Wireframing user flows for content creation and map interactions
  • High-fidelity mockups and interactive prototyping
  • Feature validation before development handoff
  • Developer collaboration for accurate implementation
